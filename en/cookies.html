Cookie policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This website does not use tracking cookies

tacticalbiscuit.com does not set advertising, analytics or social media cookies. That is why you won’t see a cookie banner when you visit.

Client app

The Team Biscuit app stores in your browser the information needed to keep you signed in and remember your preferences (for example, dark mode). This is technical storage that the app needs in order to work, and it is not used to track you or for advertising.

External services

If you tap the WhatsApp or Instagram links, you will leave this website and those services’ cookie policies will apply.

How to delete stored data

You can delete the data your browser stores at any time from its settings. If you do, you will need to sign in to the app again.
