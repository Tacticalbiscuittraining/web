FAQ

Your questions answered

Before you start

Do I need experience or to be in shape to start?

No. The plan starts from where you really are: your level, your sporting background, your schedule and the equipment you have. We begin with what you can do today and the demands increase gradually, at your pace.

What does the initial assessment involve?

It’s an interview about your goal, your experience, your daily routine and your eating habits, together with a body composition analysis. With that information I set up your profile and design a plan made just for you.

Do I have to train in a gym?

Not necessarily. Your training is adapted to wherever you’ll be working out: the gym, at home with minimal equipment, or outdoors.

Do you work with injuries or health conditions?

My work is training and sports nutrition coaching, not clinical treatment. If you have an injury or a medical condition, or take medication, I adapt the plan following your doctor’s or physiotherapist’s advice, and for the clinical side of your diet I recommend seeing a registered dietitian.

The plan and follow-up

How does online coaching work?

You get your plan in the Team Biscuit app: there you see today’s workout, your diet and your progress, and you log what you do. I see your logs as they come in, you can message me whenever you need to, and every month we do a full review to adjust the plan.

Is the diet very strict? Will I always have to eat the same thing?

No. You get specific amounts for each meal, but also equivalent alternatives to swap foods without leaving the plan, plus a ready-made shopping list. The idea is that you can stick to it in everyday life.

What if I can’t train as planned one week?

There are no fixed days: you train when you can during the week. If your schedule changes, you travel or you have a tough week, we talk it through and adjust the plan.

Do I have to take supplements?

They’re not compulsory. The foundation is training, nutrition and rest. I’ll only recommend a supplement if it fits your goal and is backed by science.

Is it suitable if I’m preparing for a race or other event?

Yes. I combine your race plan with strength work and nutrition, organised in phases right up to race day.

Terms

When will I start to see results?

It depends on your starting point, your goal and your consistency. At every review we measure your progress, so you can see how you’re doing in the data and not just in the mirror.

Is there a minimum commitment?

The monthly options have no minimum term. The “Total Change” Pack is a fixed 90-day programme.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the service and how you want to work. Message me on WhatsApp, Instagram or email and I’ll send you all the details, with no obligation.
