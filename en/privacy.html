Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you visit this website, contact Tactical Biscuit Training or use the Team Biscuit client app, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).

Data controller

Tactical Biscuit Training, Valencia (Spain). Contact: contacto@tacticalbiscuit.com.

What data is processed and why

  • Visiting the website. The website does not use forms, tracking cookies or analytics tools. The hosting service records technical connection data (such as your IP address) for security reasons.
  • Contact. If you write via WhatsApp, Instagram or email, the data you send (name, contact details and message) is used to reply to you and tell you about the services.
  • Clients and the Team Biscuit app. To provide the service, we process your account data (email), your training and nutrition plan, the logs you record (workouts, runs, meals, habits), your measurements and body composition and, if you authorise it, progress photos and health data relevant to your training.

Legal basis

  • Answering your enquiries: your consent when you write to us.
  • Providing the contracted service: performance of the contract.
  • Health data and progress photos: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who can see your data

Your client data can only be seen by you and your trainer. Progress photos are visible only to you and the trainer. No data is passed on to third parties unless required by law.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long data is kept

Contact data, for as long as the conversation lasts. Client data, for as long as the relationship continues and, afterwards, for the applicable legal periods. You can ask for your account to be deleted at any time.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to contacto@tacticalbiscuit.com. If you believe they have not been properly addressed, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
