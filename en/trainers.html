What it is

All your work, in one place

No more jumping between WhatsApp, spreadsheets, your calendar and notes. You build and follow the plans; your client has everything on their phone.

Training

Exercise library with images, sets, weights and rest. Your own templates, applied to one or several clients in one tap.

Nutrition

Nutrition plans with amounts and alternatives. Clients tick off what they eat and you see how they stick to it.

Custom check-ins

Your own weekly or monthly forms, with photos and measurements, compared with the previous check-in.

Bookings and packs

Session types, your hours, client bookings, sessions left in a pack and cancellation policy.

Chat and automatic messages

Chat with each client, saved replies and messages that go out on their own: welcome, reminders and inactive clients.

Your brand

Your name, logo and colour in your clients' app. Plus your own page to win new clients.
